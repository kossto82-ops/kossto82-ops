## 🧠 How I Approach Problems

**1. Understand the workflow**

I start with the business problem rather than the technology.

**2. Identify constraints**

I look for rules, dependencies, edge cases, failure points and repetitive manual work.

**3. Design the simplest useful system**

The goal is not to add technology. The goal is to remove friction.

**4. Use AI where it provides leverage**

AI is a tool and a force multiplier — not a substitute for understanding the problem.

**5. Validate**

I use testing, evaluation, real workflows and measurable evidence wherever possible.

**6. Iterate**

I document decisions, identify limitations and distinguish clearly between prototype, production-ready and experimental work.
